For complete details on the use and execution of this protocol, please refer to Su et al.1 BEFORE YOU BEGIN Itaconate, an electrophilic a, b-unsaturated carboxylic acid has been reported to inhibit the inflam- mation responses in many cells.2 It can alkylate protein cysteine residues by Michael addition to regulate the function of targeted proteins.3 4-Octyl itaconate (4-OI), a derivative of itaconate, also inhibits the inflammation by alkylating its targeted proteins.3 Itaconate alkyne (ITalk) is a re- ported biorthogonal probe to detect itaconate alkylation in living cells by click chemistry.4 Click chemistry was proposed as a functional form of chemistry, where molecules snap together quickly and efficiently in a mild condition.5 The one of the most important reactions of click chemistry is the copper catalyzed azide-alkyne cycloaddition (CuAAC).6,7 This reaction is widely used and improved to link molecules together in many biorthogonal methods. The key rational of the ITalk is that the long carbon chain of 4-OI remains on the target proteins after modification.4 Therefore, the long carbon chain of 4-OI can provide the site for introducing the biorthogonal handle, such as alkyne.4 Consequently, we propose that ITalk should be referred to as a 4-OI alkylation probe instead of an itaconate alkylation probe. Here, we describe a click-chemistry-based protocol for de- tecting 4-octyl-itaconate-alkylated proteins in primary bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) via probe ITalk. The protocol below describes the specific steps for using BMDMs. How- ever, we have also used this protocol in human THP-1, mouse Raw264.7 cells and mouse embryonic fibroblasts. Euthanize a C57BL/6J mouse using the approved methods of primary and secondary euthanasia at your institution. Culture bone marrow cells in a standard humidified 37C, 5% CO2 incubator. And collect cells on day 5. LIMITATIONS ITalk may be hydrolyzed by esterase in some cells, which would decrease the pulling-down efficiency of streptavidin. Replacing the ester linkage of ITalk with an amide would enhance labeling efficiency in some cells.14 In the immunoblotting experiment, we used DMSO as a control. A saturated non- reactive click probe would serve a better control. TROUBLESHOOTING Problem 1 No protein pellets after click (step 14). Potential solution Increase the protein amount used for click.                                                               Guide

                                                              You do not type anything

                                                              Select the words in the Methods text, then press Use highlighted text. The exact words you selected are what gets recorded.

                                                              Typing is what this design removes. Given a sentence like the one below, one person types RIPA, another types the whole parenthesis, a third types the clause. All three read the paper correctly and disagree in the data. Selecting removes that entirely.

                                                              The cell total protein was isolated using RIPA (Applygen Technologies Inc., Beijing, China). Protease inhibitor (CWBIO, Shanghai, China) was added into the RIPA at a ratio of 1:100.

                                                              For lysis buffer there, mark just RIPA, the first time it appears.

                                                              How much to select

                                                              The shortest phrase that states the detail. Not the whole sentence when three words will do, and not the vendor, city or catalogue number unless the question asks for them.

                                                              Every question shows a worked example under What to mark. Follow it rather than your instinct.

                                                              When a paper gives several

                                                              Many papers list a table of antibodies with eight RRIDs, eight catalogue numbers and several vendors. Mark them all. Press Use highlighted text once per value and they stack up in a numbered list. Press Done, next when the list is complete.

                                                              If they sit in a contiguous table or a run-on list, selecting the whole block in one go is fine and much faster. You are marking where the evidence is, not counting.

                                                              The same value repeated does not need marking twice. A paper that uses 1:1000 for six antibodies has one dilution, not six.

                                                              If you stop before you have them all, tick 'There are more of these than I marked'. That box matters more than it looks: without it, someone who stopped at five of eight cannot be told apart from someone who found everything, and the three they skipped would count against the extractor as mistakes it did not make.

                                                              What counts

                                                              Judge only what is written. Not what the authors almost certainly did, and not what is standard practice in the field.

                                                              Only the Methods section is shown, and that is deliberate. If a detail appears solely in a figure legend or a supplementary file, it is not in the text you are given, so the answer is Not stated.

                                                              Unclear is a real answer. It is set aside rather than counted either way, so use it when you are genuinely stuck rather than guessing.

                                                              The shaded sentences

                                                              Opening a question shades the sentences most likely to hold the answer, and Prev and Next step between them.

                                                              They are a search aid and nothing more. A crude keyword rule picks them, it knows nothing about the answer, it sometimes shades irrelevant sentences and sometimes misses the right one. Read around them. When nothing is shaded, read the section normally.

                                                              Getting through a batch

                                                              The Methods text stays pinned on the left while questions scroll on the right. Answers collapse to one line and the next question opens automatically. Click any collapsed line to change it.

                                                              Your work is saved in this browser as you go, so closing the tab loses nothing. Submit is at the top of the page and turns on once every question is answered and your initials are filled in.

                                                              RRID or antibody identifierAn RRID for an antibody, such as RRID:AB_2160010, or a bare AB_ accession number. A catalogue number on its own is not an RRID.
